This repository contains a qualitative research essay and field interview compilation examining the socio-economic challenges faced by Pashtun communities in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) and former FATA (Federally Administered Tribal Areas) in Pakistan.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Drawing from qualitative primary interviews (including residents from the Batani tribe, FATA/Ghindola, and business owners in Kalam, Swat Valley) alongside personal accounts, this paper explores two critical issues affecting everyday life in Pashtun border regions:

1. **Terrorism & Displacement:** How local populations are caught between armed non-state actors (including foreign fighters) and government operations, giving rise to harmful racial stereotypes and forced internal displacement (e.g., to Dera Ismail Khan).
2. **Recurring Disasters & Lack of Aid:** The severe impact of monsoon flooding along the Swat River and mountainous areas, where local communities must rebuild critical roads and infrastructure through mutual aid due to a lack of sustained government aid.
